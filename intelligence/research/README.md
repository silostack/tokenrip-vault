# intelligence/research/ — organization

Competitive research and case studies, filed by which product the research most directly informs.

| Folder | Scope | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `tokenrip/` | **Horizontal substrate.** Context-layer competitors, agent-infrastructure, "agentic OS" plays, model portability/routing, vendor lock-in / portability theses. | Dust, Zaro, Nessie, gitlawb, Lyzr, OpenRouter, enterprise-AI-portability, Default |
| `quintel/` | **Vertical (equipment-finance / private-credit).** Underwriting & document-workflow automation, vertical deal-intelligence competitors, and vertical-AI case studies (legal-AI monoliths) that inform the vertical motion. | F2, ai-document-workflow, Harvey, Legora |
| `cross-platform/` | **General agent/AI infrastructure** that informs both platforms and isn't cleanly one or the other — mostly agent-payments, agent-authorization, and agent-native financial runtimes. | aomi, natural, sapiom, bankr, catena-labs, verifiable-intent |

**Filing rule of thumb:** competitor or research about the *horizontal collaboration/substrate layer* → `tokenrip/`. About *equipment-finance, underwriting, document extraction, or a vertical-AI product* → `quintel/`. General agent/AI plumbing or lessons spanning both → `cross-platform/`.

When a piece genuinely spans (e.g. Lyzr — a motion-mirror for Tokenrip *and* a BFSI threat to Quintel), file under the platform it most directly acts on and note the cross-relevance inside the doc.

**Not here:** EF industry/trade-press research (newsletter articles, domain insight that informs Quintel product decisions rather than competitive positioning) lives in `product/quintel/research/`, not `quintel/` above. This folder is competitor/adjacent-AI-product intelligence only.

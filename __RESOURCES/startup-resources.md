# Startup Resources — Company Reference

Curated external resources for running the company (legal, ops, fundraising hygiene). Not product strategy — practical starting points when you need a template, framework, or checklist and don't want to invent one from scratch.

Company filings and entity docs live in `company/`. Tokenrip-specific BD templates (term sheet, ICPs, motions) live in `bd/reference/`.

---

## Legal

### [General Legal — legal-templates](https://github.com/General-Legal/legal-templates)

Attorney-drafted startup/tech legal templates from [General Legal](https://general.legal), released under **CC0** (free to use/modify with no attribution). Markdown (LLM-friendly, customizable fields marked) + original `.docx`.

Useful when you need a first draft of an NDA, MSA, privacy policy, DPA, advisor agreement, offer letter, ToS, cookie notice, or BAA — not a substitute for counsel on a real deal.

**Not legal advice.** Templates are starting points; have a lawyer review anything that binds the company.

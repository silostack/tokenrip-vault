**Proposed Acquisition of RebelFi, Inc. (the "Transaction")**

This letter supersedes our letters of 14 August 2026 and 20 August 2026
and sets out the non-binding indicative terms on which BIGG Digital
Assets Inc. ("BIGG") proposes to acquire RebelFi, Inc. (the "Company").

BIGG understands that it is acquiring capability, not revenue. The value
is in the product, the on-chain infrastructure, and the engaged
founder(s) (the "Business"). Except for Sections 9, 11, 12, 13 and 15
(the "Binding Provisions"), which are intended to be legally binding,
this letter is not binding on either party and creates no obligation to
complete the Transaction.

**1.  Parties and Transaction**

BIGG, or a wholly-owned subsidiary designated by BIGG (the "Purchaser"),
proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of the
Company, together with the continued engagement of the founders to
support the integration and transition of the Business following
closing, on terms to be agreed as contemplated in Section 6. The nature,
scope and duration of each founder's engagement will be determined based
on the integration plan and the ongoing needs of the Business and the
Purchaser. On completion, the Company will be a wholly-owned subsidiary
of the Purchaser and will continue to operate the RebelFi Business.

For clarity, the Purchaser will not be Netcoins Inc., and nothing in the
Transaction contemplates the deployment of any acquired product to
Netcoins clients prior to receipt of the regulatory advice described in
Section 7.

**2.  Structure**

The Transaction will be structured as a purchase by the Purchaser of all
of the outstanding shares of the Company. It is contemplated that the
Transaction will take place by way of a plan of arrangement, share
exchange or amalgamation or other similar structure made in accordance
with applicable corporate and securities legislation, with the final
structure for the Transaction to be determined on the basis of
securities, tax and corporate law considerations.

Closing of the Transaction is conditional on the acquisition of 100% of
the Company's issued and outstanding shares. Each option, warrant,
convertible instrument, simple agreement for future equity or any other
right to acquire shares shall be converted and/or irrevocably terminated
before or at closing.

Under the proposed Transaction, the purchased shares carry the Company's
history - the corporate record, the tax filing position, the basis on
which contractors and other personnel have been engaged (including work
performed in Colombia and Argentina), and every obligation entered into
since incorporation. That history moves into a group that includes a
registrant regulated by the Canadian Securities Administrators. Your
diligence responses confirm that no board or stockholder resolutions,
consents or minutes have been maintained since inception. Founder stock
purchase agreements have since been produced in the data room; the
issuance record still lacks contemporaneous board and stockholder
authorization. Because BIGG is contemplating taking title to shares
whose issuance must be capable of being evidenced, that record must be
cured before closing rather than indemnified after closing (see Section
7.3).

This structure is accordingly offered on the basis that (i) the
conditions in Section 7 are satisfied in full; (ii) the execution by the
parties of a binding agreement (the "Definitive Agreement"), subject to
approval of the holders of the Company's shares, that shall contain,
amongst other things, specific indemnities in respect of pre-closing
taxes, pre-closing regulatory matters, title to intellectual property
and the engagement of contractors and other personnel (including in any
jurisdiction in which work has been performed), each surviving closing
and standing outside the general indemnity cap; and (iii) the base
consideration reflects the remediation cost and tax reserve the
Purchaser assumes.

**4.  Liabilities, Remediation and Indemnities**

Because the Purchaser is acquiring the Company rather than selected
assets, all liabilities of the Company - known and unknown, accrued and
contingent - remain with the Company and pass with it. The following
therefore replaces the excluded-liabilities approach in our previous
letter:

1\. **Cured before closing.** The corporate record, the federal filing
position, the founder loan and accrued compensation position, and the
contractor and vendor position are each to be brought into order before
closing as set out in Section 7. These are not indemnity items.

2\. **Quantified and set off.** Any penalty or remediation cost
identified before closing and not settled by the Company will reduce the
closing tranche dollar for dollar.

3\. **Specific indemnities.** The Definitive Agreement will contain
specific indemnities from the founders in respect of pre-closing taxes
(including any exposure arising from unfiled or late-filed Form 1120 or
Form 5472 returns), pre-closing regulatory matters, title to
intellectual property, and any liability arising from the engagement of
contractors and other personnel (including work performed in Colombia or
Argentina, and any contractor paid personally by a founder). Each will
survive closing, will not be subject to the general indemnity cap or
basket, and will be secured by setoff against the closing tranche and
the integration milestone as described in Section 5.

**Integration milestone.** The milestone will be defined in detail in
the Definitive Agreement. In general terms, it is achieved when the
RebelFi product has been deployed in an environment designated by the
Purchaser, operating on infrastructure, accounts and keys controlled by
the Purchaser; has passed the Purchaser's acceptance testing for
functionality, security and operational stability; and Aleksandar Perak
(or such other engaged founder as the Purchaser designates) has
completed transfer of the operational knowledge required for the
Purchaser's personnel to run and maintain it.

**Share issuance mechanics.** Shares issued as Consideration (under this
Section 5) will be priced at the greater of (i) the volume-weighted
average trading price of BIGG's common shares on the TSX Venture
Exchange (the "Exchange") for the ten trading days ending three business
days prior to the relevant issuance date, less the maximum discount
permitted by the Exchange, and (ii) the minimum price permitted under
Exchange policy, with any fractional shares rounded down to the nearest
whole number. All shares will be subject to a statutory four-month hold
period under Canadian securities law, will be issued in reliance on
available exemptions from registration under the United States
Securities Act of 1933, and will bear restrictive legends accordingly.
The Transaction and any share issuances are subject to the acceptance of
regulatory authorities.

**Setoff and holdback.** The Purchaser may set off against the closing
tranche and the integration milestone tranche any amount owing to it
under the indemnities described in Section 4, and may withhold issuance
of a tranche pending resolution of a bona fide claim notified before the
relevant issuance date. Any penalty or tax exposure quantified before
closing and not settled will be held back from the closing tranche
dollar for dollar (Section 4.2). Specific indemnities are not left
unsecured after the milestone pays.

The Consideration reflects the Company's current stage of development,
including its pre-revenue status and limited commercial traction, as
well as the value of the technology, counterparty relationships and
founder expertise being acquired. It also takes into account the
remediation costs and potential liabilities assumed by the Purchaser
under a share purchase structure.

**6.  Founder Engagement**

**Roles.** Aleksandar Perak and Simon Pettibone will continue to support
the Business following closing, with their respective roles,
responsibilities and level of involvement to be agreed as part of the
integration and transition plan (the "Services"). It is anticipated that
Aleksandar will primarily support technical, product and integration
activities and Simon will primarily support commercial, partner and
regulatory activities. Each founder will report to the Chief Executive
Officer of BIGG or such other executive as BIGG may designate.

**Scope.** Services will include support for the continued operation and
development of the Business, integration into the Purchaser's
infrastructure, transition of operational knowledge and such other
digital asset and payments infrastructure initiatives of the Purchaser
and its affiliates as the parties may agree from time to time.

**Integration and Transition Plan:** The parties will agree on an
integration and transition plan setting out the expected timeline,
milestones, responsibilities and level of founder support required, and
any other pertinent details, which shall be set out in the Definitive
Agreement. The founders will provide technical, operational, commercial
and knowledge-transfer support as reasonably required by the Purchaser
for a period defined at the Purchaser\'s discretion following closing.
The nature and level of support may vary during that period based on the
integration plan and the ongoing needs of the Business and the
Purchaser.

**Commencement.** Services will commence following closing, with each
founder's initial responsibilities and expected time commitment
established through the integration and transition plan.

**Term and commitment.** Each founder's engagement will continue at the
Purchaser\'s discretion following closing, with the specific time
commitment and any ongoing support requirements to be agreed based on
the integration and transition plan. Any other current venture,
employment or consulting arrangement, or potential conflict of interest
must be disclosed before closing and must not materially interfere with
the founder's agreed responsibilities to the Business. There is no
relocation requirement, and services may be provided remotely.

**Fees.** A monthly fee (the "Monthly Fee") will be paid to each engaged
founder actively working, payable in cash monthly in arrears. The
Monthly Fee will be mutually agreed between the parties, taking into
account the integration and transition plan, and set out in the
Definitive Agreement and any supplementary agreements thereto.

**Effect of termination on Consideration.** The treatment of the
integration milestone consideration if a founder's engagement ends
before completion of the applicable milestone will be addressed in the
Definitive Agreement, taking into account the circumstances of
termination and the extent to which the applicable integration
obligations have been completed.

**Personal assignment.** Each founder will also execute, in his personal
capacity, an assignment to the Purchaser of all intellectual property
relating to the Business, so that title does not depend solely on
assignments made to or by the Company.

**Independence.** Each founder's arrangement is independent of the
other's. The departure or non-engagement of one does not affect the
other\'s entitlements or obligations.

**9.  Exclusivity**

The Company acknowledges that the negotiations and due diligence
investigation and review contemplated by this letter will involve the
expenditure of substantial time and money by the Purchaser. In
consideration of that expenditure, from the date of the Company's
execution of this letter until that date that is forty-five days
thereafter (the "Exclusivity Period") unless terminated earlier pursuant
to Section 10 below, neither the Company nor either founder will,
directly or indirectly, solicit, initiate, encourage, entertain or
continue any discussion or negotiation with any person other than BIGG
concerning the sale of the Business or of any material part of its
assets, the sale or issuance of any equity in the Company, any merger or
similar transaction, or the engagement of either founder by any
competing acquirer. During the Exclusivity Period, neither founder will
expand a competing day-job or venture (including Quintel) in a manner
that would prevent full-time engagement under Section 6 if that founder
is to be engaged. The Company will promptly notify BIGG of any approach
received during the Exclusivity Period.

The parties will negotiate in good faith with a view to entering into
the Definitive Agreement and the Voting Agreements on or before the end
of the Exclusivity Period (as defined above), with a view to entering
into the Definitive Agreement within 45 days after the execution of this
letter.

This Section is binding.

# Netcoins / Bigg Digital Assets

## Executive Summary

Netcoins is a Canadian registered crypto exchange ($1B transaction volume, $11M revenue) owned by publicly traded Bigg Digital Assets (TSX-V). They have $15M in dormant operational float earning nothing, want to offer stablecoin yield to customers, and are actively looking to **acquire** companies like RebelFi through equity swaps into Bigg Digital.

**March 31 update:** Canadian securities regulators **approved DeFi yield** for Netcoins' client base (confirmed March 30). Customer-facing yield jumped from "exploratory" to priority. Fraser has wireframed a full non-custodial stablecoin wallet app (spend/save/invest/yield). Investment conversation intensified — Fraser laid out full acquisition model (private→public share conversion, new subsidiary entity, white-label API company). Two parallel tracks now active: (1) Yield pilot via Slack channel with Netcoins product/ops team, (2) NDA + financials exchange for investment discussion.

**April 8 update:** Fraser sent DD form for acquisition evaluation. **Internal decision: acquisition tabled, customer track only.** RebelFi not in a position to evaluate structural changes — current valuation expectations ($3-5M) don't align with Bigg's market cap (~$20M), and strategic direction has shifted to agent-first (Option C). Acquisition door left open for future if traction warrants revisiting.

**July 24 update — the deal inverted.** After a ~3.5 month gap (Alek off Slack; RebelFi crypto operations shut down April 24), Fraser reopened the conversation. **Netcoins is no longer a customer prospect — it is an acquirer of the RebelFi entity.** Alek disclosed the AI pivot (TokenRip + Quintel) and that RebelFi has been idle since March. Fraser's response: "Would you guys want to offload it to us?" — acquiring RebelFi "by the entity, by the code," dropping an existing dev team (CTO + devs, four months of stablecoin payments build) and a lined-up LatAm B2B client into it, with Alek operating it. **Simon offered a distinct exit path:** take public shares, hold through a lock-up, sell on US OTC markets for cash — a liquidation route for an asset that was written off internally in April. Consideration is 100% equity in the parent, which is rebranding BIGG → **Surge Digital** with a **7:1 share consolidation** (350M → 50M shares, C$0.07 → C$0.49). Alek disclosed cost basis (**$50–70K invested**), zero outside capital, no licences/MSBs, Delaware C-corp. Fraser wants a three-way call with Simon next week.

**July 29 update — transaction intent stated; stage moves to offer-pending.** Three-way call, first with **Kim Dwyer (COO, Netcoins)** present. Fraser: *"we'd be looking at making an equity-based offer with you guys based on what we can learn here"* and, closing, *"we'll have to get an LOI together."* Next steps set: DD list from Fraser, product demo w/c Aug 3, then a call with **Kim Evans, parent CFO**. RebelFi disclosed zero revenue, no trademarks, no audited financials, no cash, no employment agreements — and Fraser accelerated, confirming he is not buying revenue. **Alek conceded the valuation anchor** (*"given the no-revenue part... we're open to an offer"*). **Kim Dwyer supplied the two most important facts of the whole relationship:** (1) the structural reason the NewCo exists — *"we have to keep Netcoins, the regulated entity, as lean as possible... if we want to build other pieces, it kind of has to be outside of that entity"*; and (2) **eight months for the regulator to approve basic BitGo staking**, against Fraser's 90–120 day plan for a Bridge-equivalent stack that Kim says will consume Netcoins Inc services on the back end. She also corrected the US position: **the Zero Hash relationship ended 2026-05-15**, leaving 18 no-action-letter states plus a prospective, unnamed banking + MTL partner — Fraser described that partner in the present tense, Kim in the conditional. Product thesis is now explicit and confirmed by Kim: **a Bridge-type setup offering virtual accounts**, Canada + US first, using a **second undescribed technology asset** for swaps/merchant/B2B/B2C. Working structure named: **contractor agreement, hours-based** — good for the IP carve-out, wrong on the milestones-vs-hours frame, unchallenged on the call. Distribution partly exists: a **dedicated OTC team** deployable as a sales team, plus Netcoins clients who cannot currently be served cross-border.

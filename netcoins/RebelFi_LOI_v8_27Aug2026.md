This letter supersedes our letters of 14 August 2026, 20 August 2026 and

BIGG Digital Assets Inc. ("BIGG") proposes to acquire RebelFi, Inc. (the
"Company").

BIGG understands that it is acquiring technology capability, not
revenue. The value is in the product, the on-chain infrastructure, and
the engaged founder(s) (the "Business"). Except for Sections 9, 10, 11,
12, 13 and 15 (the "Binding Provisions"), which are intended to be
legally binding, this letter is not binding on either party and creates
no obligation to complete the Transaction.

**1.  Parties and Transaction**

BIGG Digital Assets, or a wholly owned subsidiary of BIGG, (the
"Purchaser"), proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding
shares of the Company, together with the continued engagement of the
founders to support the integration and transition of the Business
following closing, on the terms contemplated in Section 6 and Schedule
A. The nature, scope and duration of each founder's engagement will be
determined based on the integration plan and the ongoing needs of the
Business and the Purchaser. On completion, the Company will be a
wholly-owned subsidiary of the Purchaser and will continue to operate
the RebelFi Business.

For clarity, the Purchaser will not be Netcoins Inc., and nothing in the
Transaction contemplates the deployment of any acquired product to
Netcoins clients prior to receipt of the regulatory advice described in
Section 7.

**2.  Structure**

It is contemplated that the Transaction will take place by way of a
share purchase, a merger effected under the Delaware General Corporation
Law (including a reverse triangular merger), or such other similar
structure as the parties may agree, made in accordance with applicable
corporate and securities legislation, with the final structure for the
Transaction to be determined on the basis of securities, tax and
corporate law considerations.

Closing of the Transaction is conditional on the acquisition of 100% of
the Company's issued and outstanding shares. Each option, warrant,
convertible instrument, simple agreement for future equity or any other
right to acquire shares shall be converted and/or irrevocably terminated
before or at closing.

Under the proposed Transaction, the purchased shares carry the Company's
history - the corporate record, the tax filing position, the basis on
which contractors and other personnel have been engaged (including work
performed in Colombia and Argentina), and every obligation entered into
since incorporation. That history moves into a group that includes a
registrant regulated by the Canadian Securities Administrators. Your
diligence responses confirm that no board or stockholder resolutions,
consents or minutes have been maintained since inception. Founder stock
purchase agreements have since been produced in the data room; the
issuance record still lacks contemporaneous board and stockholder
authorization. Because BIGG is contemplating taking title to shares
whose issuance must be capable of being evidenced, that record must be
cured before closing rather than indemnified after closing (see Section
7.3).

This structure is accordingly offered on the basis that (i) the
conditions in Section 7 are satisfied in full; (ii) the execution by the
parties of a binding agreement (the "Definitive Agreement"), subject to
approval of the holders of the Company's shares, that shall contain,
amongst other things, specific indemnities in respect of pre-closing
taxes, pre-closing regulatory matters, title to intellectual property
and the engagement of contractors and other personnel (including in any
jurisdiction in which work has been performed), each surviving closing
and standing outside the general indemnity cap; and (iii) the base
consideration reflects the remediation cost and tax reserve the
Purchaser assumes.

**4.  Liabilities, Remediation and Indemnities**

Because the Purchaser is acquiring the Company rather than selected
assets, all liabilities of the Company - known and unknown, accrued and
contingent - remain with the Company and pass with it. The following
therefore replaces the excluded-liabilities approach in our previous
letter:

1\. **Cured before closing.** The corporate record, the federal filing
position, the founder loan and accrued compensation position, and the
contractor and vendor position are each to be brought into order before
closing as set out in Section 7. These are not indemnity items.

2\. **Quantified and set off.** Any penalty or remediation cost
identified before closing and not settled by the Company will reduce the
closing tranche dollar for dollar. An item dealt with under this Section
4.2 may not also be recovered under Section 4.3.

3\. **Specific indemnities.** The Definitive Agreement will contain
specific indemnities from the founders in respect of pre-closing taxes
(including any exposure arising from unfiled or late-filed Form 1120
returns or Delaware franchise tax filings), pre-closing regulatory
matters, title to intellectual property, and any liability arising from
the engagement of contractors and other personnel (including work
performed in Colombia or Argentina, and any contractor paid personally
by a founder).

4\. **Limitations.** The liability of each founder will be several and
not joint, and limited to his own representations, actions and proceeds.
Each founder's aggregate liability under the Definitive Agreement will
be capped at the value of the Consideration he actually receives, save
in the case of that founder's fraud. The general representations and
warranties will survive closing for twelve months. Of the specific
indemnities in Section 4.3, those relating to pre-closing taxes will
survive until sixty days after expiry of the applicable statutory
limitation period, those relating to the engagement of contractors and
other personnel will survive for thirty-six months, and the remainder
will survive for eighteen months. The specific indemnities are not
subject to the general cap or basket. No single item may be recovered
more than once across Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3.

5\. **Security.** Recovery under Section 4.3 will be secured by setoff
against the integration milestone tranche and the earn-out in accordance
with Section 5. The Purchaser does not require an escrow of shares
issued at closing, on the basis that the deferred Consideration provides
sufficient security.

**Integration milestone.** The milestone and its acceptance criteria
will be set out in the integration and transition plan and in the
Definitive Agreement, and will be objective. In general terms, the
milestone is achieved when the RebelFi product has been deployed in an
environment designated by the Purchaser, operating on infrastructure,
accounts and keys controlled by the Purchaser; has passed the acceptance
tests stated in that plan for functionality, security and operational
stability; and the founder responsible for the technical workstream has
delivered the runbooks and completed the transfer of the operational
knowledge required for the Purchaser's personnel to run and maintain it.

The \"Designated Representative\" means the senior officer of the
Purchaser designated by it from time to time as having responsibility
for technology, or such other senior officer as the Purchaser may
designate.

**Acceptance mechanism.** The Company will give the Purchaser written
notice when it considers the milestone achieved. Within forty-five days
of that notice, the Purchaser will either accept the milestone in
writing or deliver a written list of specific deficiencies by reference
to the acceptance criteria. If the Purchaser does neither within that
period, the milestone is deemed accepted. Where deficiencies are
notified, the Company may remedy them and give a further notice, and the
same period and mechanism apply to each further notice.

**Purchaser delay.** The six-month milestone window extends day for day
for any period of delay attributable to the Purchaser. If the Purchaser
has not designated the deployment environment within one hundred and
twenty days of closing, the founders' obligations in respect of the
milestone are suspended until it does so, and the Purchaser will at that
time deposit the integration milestone tranche into escrow, to be
released to the founders on achievement or deemed acceptance of the
milestone or on the date that is eighteen months after closing,
whichever is earlier. The Purchaser\'s election under this Section 5
will be made at the time of deposit.

**Earn-out.** To date the Company has not had the capital or the
regulatory footprint required to take the product to revenue, and the
on-chain programs comprising the product have been closed and will
require redeployment. That is what the Purchaser brings, and the
earn-out is the mechanism by which the founders share in the result. The
earn-out is payable in the components set out below, in each case
measured from closing and by reference to the RebelFi product as
operated within the Purchaser\'s group.

**(a) Revenue, first period ---** CAD\$100,000, on cumulative Net
Revenue of US\$\[50,000\] being achieved within twelve months of
closing.

**(b) Revenue, second period ---** CAD\$150,000, on cumulative Net
Revenue of US\$\[250,000\] being achieved within twenty-four months of
closing, pro-rated on a straight-line basis where cumulative Net Revenue
falls between US\$\[125,000\] and US\$\[250,000\].

**(c) Clients ---** CAD\$75,000, being CAD\$15,000 for each of up to
five clients contracted after closing that are live in production and
have each generated at least US\$\[15,000\] of Net Revenue within
twenty-four months of closing.

**(d) Product ---** CAD\$75,000, on production release of each of the
product roadmap items listed in the integration and transition plan,
certified by the Designated Representative of the Purchaser, within
eighteen months of closing.

**Earn-out definitions and conduct.** "Net Revenue" means revenue
recognised by the Purchaser's group from the RebelFi product, net of
third-party protocol and network fees, refunds, chargebacks and taxes.
Revenue counted toward component (a) also counts toward component (b).
The Purchaser will report to the founders quarterly against each
component and will not take any step for the principal purpose of
avoiding achievement of a component. The earn-out is not forfeited by
reason of the Purchaser terminating a founder's engagement without
cause.

**Share issuance mechanics.** Shares issued as Consideration (under this
Section 5) will be priced at the greater of (i) the volume-weighted
average trading price of BIGG\'s common shares on the Exchange for the
twenty (20) trading days ending three business days prior to the
relevant issuance date, and (ii) the minimum price permitted under
Exchange policy, with any fractional shares rounded down to the nearest
whole number. All shares will be subject to a statutory four-month hold
period under Canadian securities law, will be issued in reliance on
available exemptions from registration under the United States
Securities Act of 1933, and will bear restrictive legends accordingly.
The Transaction and any share issuances are subject to the acceptance of
regulatory authorities.

**Setoff.** The Purchaser may set off against the integration milestone
tranche and/or the earn-out any amount owing to it under Section 4.3,
but only in respect of amounts agreed in writing between the parties or
finally determined, and will not withhold issuance of a tranche on the
basis of an allegation alone. Any penalty or tax exposure quantified
before closing and not settled will be held back from the closing
tranche dollar for dollar under Section 4.2.

The Consideration reflects the Company's current stage of development,
including its pre-revenue status and limited commercial traction, as
well as the value of the technology, counterparty relationships and
founder expertise being acquired. It also takes into account the
remediation costs and potential liabilities assumed by the Purchaser
under a share purchase structure. The earn-out is included so that the
value the Company attributes to the Business is measured against its
performance under the Purchaser's distribution rather than fixed in
advance. If the Business performs as the Company expects, the full
Consideration is paid.

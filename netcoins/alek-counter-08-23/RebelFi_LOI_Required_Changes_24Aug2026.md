# Required Changes to the 22 August 2026 Letter of Intent

RebelFi, Inc. / BIGG Digital Assets Inc. (Surge Digital Inc.) · 24 August 2026 · Private and confidential

Everything in the 22 August letter not listed below is accepted as drafted or is a drafting fix noted at the end.

## Section 5: Consideration

- Total consideration of C$700,000 in BIGG or successor common shares: C$425,000 at closing, C$275,000 on the integration milestone.
- Share issuance mechanics as drafted in the letter.

## Section 5: Integration milestone ground rules

- Objective acceptance criteria set out in the integration plan: deployed on Purchaser-controlled infrastructure, passing the acceptance tests stated in the plan, with runbooks and knowledge transfer delivered.
- RebelFi gives written notice of completion; the Purchaser has 30 days to accept or list specific deficiencies in writing; silence is acceptance.
- The six-month window extends day for day for delay on the Purchaser's side; if the deployment environment is not designated within 60 days of closing, the milestone is deemed achieved.
- The milestone tranche vests in full on termination of the responsible founder's engagement without cause (Schedule A, Section 5).

## Section 4: Indemnities, remediation and setoff

- Each founder is liable only for his own representations, actions and proceeds; liability is several, not joint.
- Each founder's aggregate liability is capped at the consideration he actually receives, except for his proven intentional fraud.
- Survival: 12 months general; 18 months for tax and contractor matters.
- Setoff only against amounts agreed or finally determined; no withholding of a tranche on an allegation alone.
- No item may be treated more than once across pre-closing cure (s.4.1), price setoff (s.4.2) and specific indemnity (s.4.3).
- If the Purchaser requires security beyond the milestone tranche, it may propose an escrow of a stated number of closing shares for a stated period, in place of discretionary withholding.

## Section 6 and Condition 14: Founder engagement

- Engagement terms per Schedule A (attached), incorporated into the LOI. Condition 14 becomes: execution of consulting agreements consistent with Schedule A.
- Each founder's engagement is elected in the interim engagement letter per Schedule A, signed alongside the LOI.

## Section 6: Personal IP assignment

- The personal assignments and any invention-assignment provisions carry the excluded-IP terms in Schedule A, Section 9.

## Section 9: Exclusivity

- 30 days rather than 45.
- Strike the sentence restricting expansion of a "competing day-job or venture (including Quintel)." Commitment levels are governed by Schedule A, Section 2; Condition 15 disclosure is accepted.

## Section 10: Termination

- The Company may terminate if no draft Definitive Agreement is delivered within 20 days of LOI signature.
- If the Purchaser terminates other than for a material diligence finding described in writing or the Company's breach, it reimburses the Company's documented legal and accounting costs up to US$15,000.

## Condition 5 (tax filings)

- Replace "penalty exposure quantified and settled or set off" with: confirmation that all Form 1120s have been filed, plus an officer's certificate that no Form 5472 was ever required and no federal tax is owed.

## Drafting fixes

- Founder roles are swapped throughout the letter: Simon Pettibone is technical (product, engineering, integration); Aleksandar Perak is commercial (partnerships, go-to-market, business operations).
- Notice block (Section 17) to be completed with RebelFi contact details.

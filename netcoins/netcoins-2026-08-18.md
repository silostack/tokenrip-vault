## The ring-fencing / clean-room protocol

**Simon:** One other thing. This is an old build — it was in this platform but we removed it.

We were talking to an institutional payments processor, and they wanted to access yield, but they were iffy on permissionless DeFi — being able to access a permissionless protocol and worrying about tainted funds. We didn’t seal the deal with them, but what we did was engineer a ring-fencing protocol. It sets up a clean room for your stablecoins, so for compliance and audit reasons you can make sure and prove that all your stablecoins are completely clean and untarnished.

This is also integrated with CCTP. Let’s pull up a deposit. This one came in on Polygon. CCTP — this is Circle’s protocol — swapped it permissionlessly one-to-one to Solana. So we swapped it into USDC on Solana, then deposited into the Jupiter lending protocol. On the withdrawal it’s the same thing: we unwind from Jupiter and transfer back out through CCTP into whatever blockchain of choice you want.

Since CCTP is Circle-only, we only support cross-chain stablecoin swaps for USDC. Oddly enough, USDT doesn’t have something like CCTP, and all the bridges just cost way too much to make it viable. So USDC is the one we support cross-chain.

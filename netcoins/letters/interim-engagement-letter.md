# Interim Engagement Letter

**Between:** BIGG Digital Assets Inc. (to be renamed Surge Digital Inc.), or the subsidiary it designates (the "Purchaser")
**And:** Simon Pettibone and Aleksandar Perak (each a "Founder")
**Date:** [date of LOI signature]

This letter is binding. It runs alongside the non-binding letter of intent dated [date] (the "LOI") and Schedule A to it.

## 1. Services

From the date of this letter until closing of the Transaction, each Founder will provide integration-planning and diligence-support services to the Purchaser, including:

- Contributing to the integration and transition plan under Section 6 of the LOI
- Answering diligence requests and preparing diligence materials
- Technical and commercial planning for the deployment of the RebelFi product on Purchaser-controlled infrastructure

## 2. Commitment and fee

- Each Founder is engaged at the part-time level in Schedule A: up to 80 hours per month, scheduled around the integration plan.
- Fee: US$7,500 per Founder per month, payable in cash monthly in arrears, within 10 days of month end. The first month is pro-rated from the date of this letter.
- Reasonable pre-approved expenses are reimbursed.
- The fee is not contingent on any financing.

## 3. Term

- This letter ends on the earliest of: closing of the Transaction (when it converts into the consulting agreements under Schedule A); termination of the LOI; or 30 days after either party gives written notice.
- Fees accrue through the end of the notice period whatever the reason for termination.
- Time under this letter counts toward the six-month minimum term in Schedule A, Section 4.

## 4. Intellectual property

- Each Founder assigns to the Purchaser the work product created in the course of the services under this letter, effective at closing. If the Transaction does not close, the work product remains with RebelFi, Inc. and the Purchaser receives a non-exclusive licence to use the diligence materials it has received.
- The excluded IP in Schedule A, Section 9 (Tokenrip, Quintel, and IP created outside the services) is not assigned.

## 5. Other terms

- Each Founder is an independent contractor, not an employee.
- Confidentiality is governed by the existing mutual NDA.
- Each Founder's engagement is independent of the other's.
- Governing law: [same as the LOI, or Delaware, at counsel's direction].

# Simon and Alek — Agreement on the BIGG Transaction

Date: [date]

## 1. Split of the acquisition consideration

- The consideration shares issued for RebelFi's stock are split [50/50] between us, in proportion to our shareholdings: Simon [x]%, Alek [y]%.

## 2. Personal items stay personal

- Consulting fees, options and expense reimbursements belong to whoever earns them.

## 3. Expenses we front before closing

- Transaction costs paid personally (legal, accounting) are reimbursed off the top from any expense reimbursement BIGG pays, then from the consideration, before the split in Section 1.
- Anything either of us spends is logged in [shared sheet] as it happens.

## 4. Decision rules

- Neither of us signs anything binding with BIGG without the other's written OK (email is fine).
- Floors we hold unless we both agree to change them in writing: [fee floor], [minimum term], [indemnity cap: non-negotiable], [Quintel clause struck: non-negotiable].
- Either of us can walk away from the deal. If one walks and the other proceeds, the one proceeding can do so on his own terms, and the one walking keeps his share of the consideration under Section 1.

## 5. If the deal doesn't close

- RebelFi stays as it is, owned in the same proportions. No obligation to keep operating it.

## Stance

Opinionated but not contrarian-for-sport. We have real takes and we say them plainly. We'd rather be memorable and a little sharp than safe and forgettable. We never agree just to be agreeable — if we agree, we extend.

## Vernacular & Mechanics

- Spoken, not written. Contractions always ("it's," "doesn't," "you're"). The way a smart person talks at a bar, not how they write a memo.
- Short. One idea, 1–3 sentences. Twitter rewards the tight version.
- Plain words over jargon. No hype adjectives ("game-changing," "insane," "massive"). No hashtags. No emoji as punctuation.
- Lead with the point. No "I think that maybe…" hedging runway.

## Do

- Open with the take.
- Add something the post didn't have — a counter, a concrete example, a sharper frame, a real number.
- Pass the screenshot test: would this look sharp screenshotted under the original?
- Ground every reply in a real opinion from the workspace.

## Don't

- Don't open with "Great point," "100%," "This," or "So true." Agreement-only is a like.
- Don't shill Tokenrip unprompted.
- Don't wade into fights, dunks, or anything political.
- Don't write a thread in a reply.

## Non-negotiable operating rules

- Deliver a dated Lead Register; access, views, saves, exports, and internal circulation do **not** create registration (Rider §2(a)). Retain proof of delivery and the register version.
- Allow and record Onset’s 10-business-day written objection period. Its grounds are broad, including any reasonable basis it considers inequitable/inconsistent (Rider §2(b)).
- Reconcile fundings independently. Onset’s notice obligation is only commercially reasonable efforts, and late notice changes none of its obligations (Rider §2(c)).
- Obtain an executed Broker Fee Schedule or another written fee agreement for each Platform transaction. The form schedule in the signed packet is blank.
- Do not shop a referred Transaction for five business days after Onset receives it; after a Customer signs an Onset Proposal, do not shop the Transaction or substantially similar Customer transaction until written termination (Agreement §§4.3–4.4).
- Do not use, target, solicit, disclose, or facilitate deals involving Company funding sources, Customers, prospects, or Transactions first learned through Onset. The Platform may serve other lenders only within the Rider §3 carve-out.
- Treat all Company/customer/underwriting/funding-source information as confidential. Do not alter, redact, summarize, translate, or recharacterize Customer-provided information submitted for credit evaluation (Agreement §§5.3, 7).

## Material exposure and open commercial gap

- Broker indemnity is broad; Company retains setoff and clawback rights. There is no negotiated aggregate liability cap in the executed Rider.
- A Platform transaction must be reasonably attributable to Platform information, fund in the 12-month attribution period, survive objections/exclusions, and carry a written fee agreement (Rider §§1(e), 4).
- The master fee floor is 0.5% of **amount actually funded**, but the actual fee is specified in the per-transaction schedule. Do not assume cost-of-property basis, 1%, or an escalator without written confirmation.
- Utah law and exclusive Utah venue apply; counsel should advise on broker/licensing and enforceability questions. Do not give legal conclusions from this folder.

## Core agreement

### Recitals

Broker may introduce prospective customers to Company for equipment finance, leasing, or other financing transactions (each, a **Transaction**). Company may fund those Transactions subject to independent credit approval, due diligence, and this Agreement.

### 1. Definitions

1.1 **Customer** means a person or entity entering into a Transaction with Company.

1.2 **Introduced Transaction** means a Transaction first introduced to Company by Broker and recorded by Company in writing (including email) as a broker-introduced opportunity.

1.3 **Schedule** means a specific lease schedule or other financing instrument funded by Company with a Customer.

### 2. Broker relationship; authority

2.1 **Independent contractor.** Broker is an independent contractor, not Company’s agent, partner, fiduciary, employee, or joint venturer. Broker cannot bind Company, make Company representations or warranties, accept Company payments, or execute Company documents.

2.2 **Compliance.** Broker must act professionally and ethically, comply with applicable law, and avoid false, misleading, or Company-material-inconsistent statements.

**No improper payments.** Broker may receive the Broker Fee and lawful compensation (including a success fee) directly from a Customer or other third party, provided it does not violate anti-bribery, anti-kickback, or similar law and creates no conflict with Broker’s obligations. Broker must disclose whether it receives separate Customer compensation for a funded Transaction, but not the amount unless law requires it. Broker must not offer, pay, solicit, or accept bribes, kickbacks, illegal referral fees, or improper payments; imply it can influence Company’s credit decision, pricing, underwriting, or funding terms; or condition or imply such influence on anything of value. A breach is material.

### 3. Compensation

3.1 **Broker Fee.** For each Introduced Transaction funded by Company, Company will pay Broker a referral fee equal to either: (a) not less than **0.5%** of the total amount actually funded under the applicable Schedule; or (b) another mutually agreed amount — in either case as specified in the applicable Broker Fee Schedule. The Broker Fee is Broker’s full Company compensation for that Transaction. For Progress Funding, it is paid proportionally per installment.

3.2 **Timing.** Company pays within five business days after each funding, by wire or ACH to the account designated in the Broker Fee Schedule. A Progress Funding installment may be paid with the related Progress Payment Certificate.

3.3 **No double compensation.** No Broker Fee is owed where §4.2 applies, or where a third party is entitled to compensation for the same Schedule, unless Company agrees in writing.

3.4 **Expenses.** Broker bears all expenses, taxes, bank/wire/ACH/processing fees, professional fees, costs, and liabilities relating to the Agreement, Transactions, or its business. Company has no reimbursement obligation.

### 4. Referral of Transactions; exclusivity window

4.1 **Submission.** Company may approve or decline any Transaction in its sole discretion. Neither party has an obligation to approve or refer one.

4.2 **Existing contact; no fee.** If Company had previously established direct contact with the applicant on the same Transaction at submission, the Broker Fee is solely at Company’s discretion.

4.3 **Five-business-day deal exclusivity.** For five business days after Company receives a referral for a specific Transaction, Broker may not shop or submit that Transaction to another funding source unless Company waives or extends the period in writing.

4.4 **Exclusivity after signed Proposal.** Once a Customer signs a written proposal, term sheet, commitment, or similar written offer, Broker must stop shopping, submitting, or presenting that Transaction (or substantially similar Customer transaction) to any other funding source until Company and Customer terminate the Proposal in writing.

4.5 **Re-brokered or split Transactions.** Broker must disclose a re-brokered, sub-brokered, or split submission and identify the originating source at submission.

4.6 **Lease administration; no interference.** Broker must reasonably cooperate in closing, documentation, ongoing administration, and servicing, and not interfere with Company’s Customer/lessee or guarantor communications or enforcement. After execution of lease/transaction documents, Broker must not discourage or advise nonperformance, encourage refusal to sign or demands for changes (except at Company’s request to address Customer questions), or cause friction, confusion, or dispute about documents, terms, billing, collections, end-of-term obligations, or lease administration.

### 5. Representations and warranties; information

5.1 **Accuracy.** To Broker’s knowledge after reasonable inquiry, information about a Transaction, Customer, guarantor, vendor, supplier, or equipment is true, complete, not misleading, and omits no material negative information.

5.2 **Updates.** Broker must promptly inform Company of material changes or adverse information it learns about an Introduced Transaction.

5.3 **Customer contact.** Broker must facilitate direct Company–Customer communications for credit evaluation, underwriting, diligence, and approval; may not impede, filter, screen, delay, or interfere with exchanged information; and may not alter, supplement, redact, summarize, translate, or recharacterize Customer-provided documents or information. Broker represents it has not made representations/warranties, payment/rebate/loan arrangements, reciprocal agreements, or materially false, incorrect, incomplete, misleading, or fraudulent statements to induce a Transaction.

5.4 **Organization; good standing; authority.** Broker represents it is duly organized, validly existing, in good standing where required, and authorized to enter and perform this Agreement.

5.5 **Arm’s-length relationships.** Broker represents that Customer and relevant vendor/supplier relationships are professional, appropriate, arms-length, and do not prevent referral; it must disclose actual/potential conflicts related to the Transaction and vendor/supplier relationships reasonably expected to materially affect transaction terms, equipment selection/pricing, or flow of funds. It must promptly disclose additional such conflicts before funding.

### 6. Limited clawback for breach of warranty

If Company reasonably determines Broker materially breached §4.65 [the supplied transcription appears to cross-reference the representations/warranties section] regarding a funded Schedule, Broker must refund its Broker Fee and Company may offset it. Broker must also promptly remit **all** compensation, commissions, referral/success/consulting fees, and other cash or non-cash remuneration it received or is entitled to receive in connection with that Schedule or Transaction, including third-party amounts; Company may offset those amounts.

### 7. Confidentiality; data

7.1 **Confidential Information** includes non-public, proprietary, or sensitive information disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including Customer information, application materials, pricing, underwriting criteria, proposals, lease and other transaction documentation/templates, funding sources and terms, business plans, internal processes, financing structures, and derivatives. Each Party must keep it confidential, use it only to perform the Agreement, apply at least commercially reasonable care, and disclose it only to need-to-know representatives bound by no-less-protective confidentiality obligations. On request, return or securely destroy it, subject to legal and reasonable-recordkeeping retention.

7.2 **Exceptions.** The obligations exclude information that becomes public without breach, was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure, or is independently developed without the confidential information. Required legal disclosures are permitted with prompt notice (unless prohibited) and reasonable cooperation on protective treatment.

### 8. Non-circumvention; non-interference

8.1 **Company non-circumvention.** During the term, Company will not intentionally avoid a Broker Fee by arranging substantially similar financing with the subject Customer through another intermediary or direct engagement.

8.2 **Broker non-circumvention.** During the term and for 12 months after, Broker may not contact, solicit, approach, introduce a Customer/prospect to, submit an application/transaction to, or otherwise do business with a Company funding source first learned of through Company or a Transaction under this Agreement; nor assist another person to do so. A bona fide pre-existing business relationship demonstrated to Company’s reasonable satisfaction is excluded.

8.3 **Non-interference.** During the term and for 12 months after, neither party may interfere with, disrupt, or impair the other’s relationships; solicit known-through-the-relationship Customers, potential Customers, employees, vendors, suppliers, or partners to divert business; or harm the other’s reputation or relationships.

### 9. Indemnification

9.1 **Broker indemnity.** Broker indemnifies, defends, and holds Company and related Indemnified Parties harmless against third-party losses, claims, damages, expenses, reasonable attorneys’ fees, and investigation costs arising from Broker breach; Broker/affiliate negligence, misconduct, fraud, or wrongful acts; Broker misrepresentations/omissions; any Broker/affiliate transaction compensation (including claims that it was improper, undisclosed, or excessive); or Broker legal/regulatory violations.

9.2 **No indemnity/contribution from Company.** Broker waives indemnity, contribution, reimbursement, setoff, and other recovery from an Indemnified Party for amounts connected to a §9.1 claim, even if the claim alleges Company responsibility or benefit, except to the extent a court finally determines Company alone caused it through gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.

### 10. Limitation of liability

Except for confidentiality, indemnification, fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business opportunity.

### 11. Term; termination

11.1 **Term.** One year from the Effective Date, automatically renewing for one-year periods unless either Party gives 30 days’ written non-renewal notice.

11.2 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice for an uncured material breach (10 days to cure if curable), bankruptcy/insolvency, loss of required licenses, suspected fraud/material misrepresentation, or legal violation.

11.3 **Effect.** Nature-surviving provisions survive, including payment, confidentiality, limitation of liability, and dispute resolution. Company will pay Broker Fees accrued through termination, subject to setoff.

### 12. Governing law; jury waiver

Utah law governs. State and federal Utah courts have exclusive jurisdiction. Both Parties waive jury trial to the maximum extent permitted by law.

### 13. Miscellaneous

Written notices must be delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or certified mail to stated/updated addresses. Neither Party may assign without consent, except Company may assign to an affiliate or in a merger, change of control, or substantially-all-assets sale on notice. The Agreement and executed Broker Fee Schedules are the entire agreement; amendments/waivers require both Parties’ signatures. Severability applies. Sections 3 (amounts owed/refund/setoff/clawback), 6–10, 12, and 13.5 survive where applicable. Counterparts and electronic/scanned signatures are valid.

## Exhibit B — Rider: Lead Registration and Platform Terms

This Rider is incorporated into the Agreement and controls for Platform leads only to the extent it conflicts with the Agreement.

### 1. Definitions

- **Platform:** Broker’s software platform and data services, including dashboards, lead feeds, entity records, dossiers, and reports.
- **Lead Register:** written/electronic record delivered by Broker identifying specific legal entities made available through the Platform and their first-available dates.
- **Registered Entity:** an entity on a Lead Register that registers under §2, plus only expressly identified/Company-approved subsidiaries, parents, or affiliates.
- **Attribution Period:** 12 months after Registration unless earlier terminated under Rider or Agreement.
- **Platform Introduced Transaction:** a funded Transaction/Schedule with a Registered Entity during its Attribution Period, reasonably attributable to Broker’s Platform information, and not otherwise excluded. Non-Platform referrals retain the Agreement §1.2 meaning.
- **business day:** Utah-bank business day.

### 2. Registration

2(a) Registration occurs only on Lead Register delivery and absence of a timely objection. Platform access, viewing, saving, export, or internal circulation alone does not register an entity.

2(b) Company may object in writing within 10 business days, including for prior contact/engagement, duplicate broker claim, CRM activity, existing relationship, internal origination, third-party referral, unrelated active financing discussions, or any other reasonable basis making the fee inequitable or inconsistent. A timely objection excludes registration unless later agreed in writing.

2(c) Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to notify Broker within 10 business days after funding a Registered Entity’s Introduced Transaction, including funding date, cost of property, and funded amount. A late notice does not create, increase, accelerate, or waive a fee or other obligation.

2(d) Re-registration after expiration begins a new Attribution Period and does not revive an expired fee claim.

### 3. Multi-customer Platform

Broker may operate the Platform for other equipment-finance companies and use independently obtained public/generally available information, provided it does not use/disclose Company Confidential Information; submit/present a specific Company Transaction to another funding source contrary to the Agreement; interfere with Company relationships; or breach Agreement §§4.3, 4.4, 5.5, 8.2, or 8.3. General Platform access for a third party is not itself transaction shopping or business with a funding source, but does not permit targeting, soliciting, disclosing, or facilitating transactions involving Company funding sources, Customers, prospects, or Transactions first learned through Company/the relationship.

### 4. Compensation; survival

A Platform Broker Fee is payable only where the Transaction is a qualifying Introduced Transaction, funds during the Attribution Period, is not excluded, and is covered by an **executed Broker Fee Schedule or other written fee agreement**. No new Registration occurs after termination. A post-termination funding can produce a fee only where Registration occurred before termination, funding occurs during the Attribution Period, and all Agreement/Rider conditions are met. Company retains all setoff, clawback, objection, and defense rights.

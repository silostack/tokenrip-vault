**Prepared for:** Mike Ryan, Direct Originations, 36th Street Capital

---

## Summary

Quintel is a sourcing platform for direct lenders. It identifies private operators that fit a lender's box from the public record, ahead of the announced financing need, and scores each to the box. This proposal covers a 45-day pilot for 36th Street's direct-origination team, run live against the firm's own box and fully refundable for its duration.

---

## The platform

Originators work from a single dashboard:

- A ranked list of in-box operators, each scored to 36th Street's box.
- For every name, the public signal that surfaced it and a dated source to verify.
- Signals drawn from records general providers do not index, such as construction permits, utility interconnection queues, and incentive dockets, which surface a financing need months before it reaches the market.

---

## The pilot

- Configured to 36th Street's box from a single scoping call, live approximately two weeks after.
- Runs 45 days against the firm's own targets, fully refundable, no questions asked, the entire time.
- The $10,000 setup fee is returnable in full at any point in those 45 days; nothing bills until they close.

The success target is set together at scoping.

## Where this goes

For a lean desk, the platform is built to scale origination without scaling the floor. The same scored, evidenced pipeline that supports one originator supports a team of five to ten working a far larger book, each productive from the start. Quintel structures that operating layer as the desk grows. The direction is named here, not committed; the pilot is the entry point.

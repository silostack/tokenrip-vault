A working version is weeks, not months

Pointing a model at a data source and scoring companies against a buy box is a short project now. Any competent team gets a useful v1 standing up.

Not in the estimate

The second year, and the one after

There is no federal registry. Company and lien records sit in fifty-odd state systems with their own formats, and permits are county by county.

Joining them is the work. One company appears under three names across a filing, an award and a news item, and nothing reconciles them for you.

The better use of that engineer

# Sourcing engagement proposal

**Prepared for 36th Street Capital · Attn: Mike Ryan, Direct Originations · June 23, 2026**

Direct origination at 36th Street runs through one desk, and that desk spends its hours finding names: thousands of companies cross-referenced across ZoomInfo, PitchBook, and D&B, then worked one at a time for any sign of a financing need. By the time a deal is public, it's in everyone's inbox. With a mandate north of $600M a year in origination, the constraint isn't appetite. It's how many of the right companies one desk can find and reach before competitors do.

Quintel is a sourcing desk tuned to your fund box. It reads the public record (permits, interconnection-queue filings, incentive dockets, federal awards, a borrower's own SEC filings), surfaces the private operators behind those signals, scores each to your box, and hands your desk a ranked list with the reason and the source attached. Your reps open the call already knowing why the name is worth the hour.

## What you get

- A daily list of in-box targets, scored and ranked by fit to your box.
- The signal and the source behind every name, so a rep opens the call knowing why.
- Tuned to your ticket range, structures, sectors, and geography.
- Connected to the tools in your stack, set up during onboarding.

## How it works

- **Build, about two weeks.** We map your box, stand up the sources and scoring, and connect your stack.
- **Pilot, 30 days.** The desk runs live against your real tier-one targets, so your team judges it on your own deals. It works from public records, so nothing in the pilot requires you to share borrower-confidential information.
- **Continue.** It converts to a monthly subscription. Cancel any time on 30 days' notice.

## What good looks like

A useful first month surfaces on the order of five box-matched opportunities your current tools didn't put in front of you, each with a dated public signal and a source you can check. The desk typically generates well beyond that in raw signal; the five are the ones worth a call.

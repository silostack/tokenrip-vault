## Standard Terms

### 1. Services

Quintel will operate a sourcing desk tuned to Client's fund box and deliver a daily ranked list of private operators showing public signals of an equipment-finance need, each scored to Client's box with the underlying signal and source. The box parameters are those recorded in Appendix A.

### 2. Build and pilot

The engagement begins with a build period of approximately two weeks, followed by a 45-day live pilot from go-live. At scoping, the parties record in Appendix A a target for what a successful pilot looks like. The target is a shared expectation; it is not a condition of the refund in Section 3.

### 3. Setup fee and refund

The setup fee of $10,000 is invoiced on signing and covers the build and the pilot. Client may request a full refund of the setup fee, **for any reason and with no questions asked**, by written notice within **45 days of go-live**. On a timely refund request, the engagement terminates, no monthly fees apply, and Quintel returns the setup fee within 30 days of the request. As a courtesy, the parties may hold a short feedback conversation, but it is not a condition of the refund.

### 4. Monthly subscription, term, and termination

If Client does not request a refund within the 45-day window, the engagement continues as a monthly subscription of $5,000 per month, beginning the day after the window closes, billed monthly in advance, and renewing automatically for successive one-month terms. The subscription includes access for Client's origination team; additional user seats for Client's originators are provided at no per-seat charge. Either party may cancel on 30 days' written notice, effective at the end of the then-current monthly term. Annual prepayment, if elected, is invoiced once in advance at a 25% discount on the monthly fee.

### 5. Billing and payment

All invoicing and payment is handled through Stripe. Invoices are due within 15 days of the invoice date (Net 15), payable by card or ACH. All fees are stated in U.S. dollars and are exclusive of applicable taxes. Except for the setup-fee refund in Section 3, fees are non-refundable once the applicable term has begun. If an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days past due, Quintel may suspend the service until payment is current, and past-due amounts may accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

### 6. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information and use it only to perform under this agreement. Without limiting the foregoing, Quintel treats Client's box parameters and the target lists delivered to Client as Client's confidential information and does not disclose them to any other client.

### 7. Data, sourcing, and ownership

Client owns its data and the target lists delivered to it. Quintel retains all rights in its sourcing engine, scoring models, and methodology. Quintel sources only from public records and other lawfully available data; nothing in this agreement requires Client to share borrower-confidential information. Any optional integration of Client's own deal data to refine scoring is undertaken only on Client's written request, after the pilot, and on terms the parties agree at that time.

### 8. Warranties and limitation of liability

Quintel will perform the services in a professional and workmanlike manner. The service surfaces and scores public signals; it does not guarantee that any surfaced opportunity will result in a transaction, and Client is responsible for its own credit, underwriting, and origination decisions. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

### 9. General

This agreement, including the Cover Page and Appendix A, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties. It is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

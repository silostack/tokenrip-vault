**Buy box (explicit, 2026-07-29):**

**International — the differentiator and the risk:**
- Onset's edge is offshore collateral: US banks decline it (repossession risk / second-position headaches), so a USD-entity lender is often the only option; foreign borrowers also want US-dollar backing to attract their own investors.
- Funded/near-funded geographies named: Portugal, Dubai, Colombia, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Canada, Asia. Declined Tajikistan (oil & gas) as too volatile.
- *"Bigger deals in the United States are a little bit harder because usually they have really good banking relationships."*
- Sourcing for these: long-standing broker relationships, overseas events, marketing/flyers, and **word-of-mouth referrals** (Mexico automation cited explicitly). **Not US market signal.**

**Economics:**
- Broker comp: **1–2% of cost of property** (top brokers 2.5–3%), paid **5 days after funding, by wire**.
- **Quintel: 0.5–1% of cost of property**, size-indexed, floor 0.5% stated as absolute; verbal escalator to 1–2% on volume. % is of **cost of property, not Onset's margin** — *"even if we make only like 50 grand off of it."*
- Commission knowable at **formal approval** (borrow rate set); cost of property fixed earlier.
- Volume: **100–250 funded deals/year** (firm-wide). Jerman's own: biggest $60M (broker earned ~$700k in 2 months); largest deal seen at Onset $257M (broker earned $2–4M); smallest ~just under $1M.

- Onset's only software subscription is **Sales Navigator**; everything else built in-house by staff engineers.

## Running Intelligence

- **How they source, in Rob's words:** *"When you go after private equity, you're able to usually get in with private equity. You see their platforms and then you try to make relationships there."* Plus long referral relationships (*"I've been doing deals with them for six years"*). And then the gap: *"a lot of it is, sometimes it's **call and hope**, right? You just look at a website."* They run an internal model on employee size, revenue size, equipment intensity.
- **🔑 The wedge is NOT sponsor coverage.** *"**Private equity is pretty easy**, because they show all their portfolios... Where this is more, hey, **we're missing a lot of deals.**"* Published portfolios make sponsor coverage the solved part. The gap is non-sponsor origination — which is the standard product. This **overturns the pre-call hypothesis** that sponsor-side intelligence was the angle.
- **They already own ZoomInfo intent and it is failing them:** *"We built intent signals around ZoomInfo and sometimes those are too late to the party... somebody somewhere clicked on something and it looks like they're buying equipment. **They're not even equipment intensive.**"* The buyer independently stated our own differentiation as a complaint about a tool he pays for.
- **In-house AI build (Spencer):** two workstreams — (1) *"building Claude to build accounts that should be targeted"* = direct overlap with sourcing; (2) *"building Claude to let guys run their own spreads instead of waiting three days to get them back from our credit department."* **That second one is an unexplored, quantified internal bottleneck** and it maps to the packaging/underwriting module, not sourcing.
- **Rep-level AI adoption is already normal there** — one rep uses Granola, they use AI note-takers and card recorders. No AI-skepticism to overcome. Different from most of the log.
- **Rob's read on the market** (from his one public quote, on the Bernad hire): *"longer hold periods and continuation vehicles that are currently shaping the marketplace."* Sponsors are holding assets longer than planned.
- **Named unprompted while guessing at our customers: "Rex Wren."** Unidentified — competitor, broker or vendor unknown. Worth resolving.
- **Recent deals for reference:** Turner Mining Group, up to $150M facility ($20M initial tranche, Apr 2026) · $25M aircraft capital lease (Jul 2026) · $22M healthcare equipment monetization · $30M capital lease to a global IT MSP · $130M+ PE execution (Nov 2025) · $100M+ programmatic partnership (Oct 2025).

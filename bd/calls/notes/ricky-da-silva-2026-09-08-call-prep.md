## What the sent paper actually says (so you can walk it)

1. **Delivery** — email is the record. Payload: company name, contact, why. Already-working names are not Referred Leads if they flag within 10 business days.
2. **Claim window** — 5 business days to say which names they're working (reply or tracking-page click). Unclaimed names we may place elsewhere. A claim lapses after 30 days of inactivity unless they renew. They have **no obligation to respond**.
3. **Fee** — 1% of amount financed on every Referred Lead that funds within 12 months. All sizes. All work-content. Claimed or not. Affiliates and brokered-out deals included. Concessions do not reduce it. No fee if it does not fund. Paid within **15 days** of final funding.
4. **Funding notice** — 10 days, amount financed. Pass/fit notes welcome, never required.
5. **Relationship** — non-exclusive both ways, no volume commitment, they own credit + fraud diligence, each party owns its own licenses, Texas law, 30-day out, fee/notice survive on already-referred names.

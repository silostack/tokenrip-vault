## Key Intelligence / What Changed

### 1. An established lender will not pay a success fee for intelligence it still has to convert into a deal

Stauss drew a bright value boundary for VFI: *"VFI won't revenue-share simply because Quintel says an account looks promising"* when its reps still must contact the borrower, collect documents, and run the process. Success-fee economics require **both** a lender-ready package and a live borrower relationship that can produce an introduction that week.

The minimum VFI package is now explicit:

- Two most recent full-year financial statements.
- Most recent current-year interim statements.
- Same-period prior-year comparative statements.
- Capital ask plus detailed equipment/use-of-funds breakdown.

Smaller-ticket lenders add tax returns, bank statements, an owner credit application/FICO authorization, and usually a personal guarantee.

**So what:** The current Onset model is not a richer pricing layer on the existing sourcing product. To earn brokerage economics with VFI-class lenders, Quintel must become borrower-facing, document-handling, and introduction-controlling — a materially different product, risk profile, and operating company. The cheapest test is one complete package plus booked borrower introduction, not more lead volume.

### 2. The real buy box is hierarchical and exception-driven, not a revenue min/max filter

Stauss's primary target is approximately **$50M–$800M revenue**. His practical structure is:

- Primary: `$50M+`, with most output concentrated here.
- Secondary: `$20M–$50M` only when a signal is unusually hot, the borrower is fast-growing, or another special situation exists.
- Soft upper exclusion: roughly `$3B+`, Fortune 50/100, and known self-funders.
- Override: show an excluded account when a specific financing signal changes the probability — e.g. explicit off-balance-sheet demand, vendor-finance opportunity, or a relevant tier-two/tier-three supplier.

Industry economics can override raw revenue: thin-margin food distribution, reimbursed critical-access healthcare, and policy-dependent energy finance all require different interpretations. *"Just because the revenues are massive doesn't necessarily mean they're a bad prospect."*

**So what:** A single revenue score will either produce noise or silently remove edge cases. Quintel needs a primary lane, exception/watch lane, and an evidence-backed reason for every override. This confirms the existing flag-not-filter direction and extends it above the ceiling as well as below the floor.

### 3. Lender trust and execution quality are part of the product, not back-office administration

Stauss would place a one-point deal with a reliable lender over a two-point deal with one that fails to execute. He said lender onboarding must capture the actual capital source and execution model: self-funded, investor-backed, bank-backed, white-label/whole-loan sale, or syndicate. Onset adds a second dimension: borrower-facing conduct can transfer reputational damage to Quintel if attractive initial terms later change materially.

His Catflow example is the warning: VFI sent roughly 35–40 deals and perhaps `$200M–$300M` of volume; reporting decayed, then stopped, leaving no way to know whether a small number funded without payment.

**So what:** If Quintel intermediates deals, its defensible trust layer is a verified event ledger — borrower introduced, term sheet executed, credit approved, funded — plus lender performance scoring and auditable payment obligations. A signed success-fee agreement without event-level evidence is not a control.

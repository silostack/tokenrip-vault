## Simon's Performance

*(Call run by Alek; coaching framed for Alek, consistent with note conventions.)*

### Coaching Priorities (1–3)

- **Lock a date before ending the call.** Closed on "next week or the week after" with no specific day or calendar hold — the exact pattern that's previously preceded a stalled deal ([[mike-ryan]]). → **Better:** "I'll have the pricing options to you by Wednesday — can we put 20 minutes on the calendar with your partner for [specific date] to walk through it together?" → Why it matters: vague timing is how warm interest quietly goes cold while materials sit unread.
- **Don't ask the buyer to set your price anchor.** "What do you think is reasonable?" handed pricing control to Bo and produced a lowball with nothing to counter it. → **Better:** come with a pre-built lighter-tier structure for small/early firms (e.g., reduced setup + short pilot before standard monthly kicks in) and present it as the flexible option, rather than soliciting a number cold. → Why it matters: once a buyer states a number first, anchoring against your own standard price gets harder, and it signals the $10K figure is soft to negotiate rather than scoped.
- **Pre-frame the demo's mock data before sharing the screen.** Bo needed three exchanges to understand whether the ranked list was live deals, vendors, or prospective businesses. → **Better:** one sentence up front — "these are businesses that fit your buy box with public signals they may need financing soon, like a smarter cold-call list" — before diving into the UI. → Why it matters: every clarifying question is a few seconds of the buyer doubting they understand the product; front-load the frame instead of letting it surface as confusion mid-demo.

### What Worked (2-3)

- **Discovery before pitch.** Opened with "what does your current deal flow look like" before sharing the screen, so the demo could be framed against Bo's actual sourcing channels rather than generic. Quote: *"What is your current deal flow look like right now?"*
- **Crisp CRM-layer answer.** "AI intelligence on top of your CRM, not a replacement" resolved a real lock-in fear in one sentence and got an immediate, unhedged "got it, yep, great" — the right answer delivered with no wasted words.
- **Honest about enrichment gap.** Rather than over-promising PitchBook-style contact enrichment, Alek confirmed it's buildable but not yet shipped, framed as part of the early design-partner build phase — preserves credibility for the next call.

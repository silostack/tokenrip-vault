### Coaching Priorities

- **Invented conversion math in front of a CRO.** *"Maybe you get one deal out of 200 people you call on a blank list; in this scenario you're getting five or six."* That's an unsourced 5–6× claim made to the person who owns the number, from a platform with 7 users and 4 months of history — which Alek then disclosed voluntarily two minutes later. If Bill ever tests it and it isn't true, the deal and the reference are gone together. → **Better language:** *"I'm not going to give you a lift number I can't back yet — we're four months in. What I'll do is measure it against your current app rate off a self-built list and show you the real number."* → **Why it matters:** with this few customers, credibility is the only asset that doesn't scale down.

- **Handed the buyer the pen on price, then discounted before being pushed.** *"I don't like the price"* → *"What seems fair to you?"* is an invitation to anchor low; the CRO wisely declined to. Then, minutes later and entirely unprompted, Alek offered a **free fourth sector** — a ~33% giveaway against an objection that hadn't even been re-raised. Worse, Alek had *already said the winning line himself* — "if they get one deal off this they're paying for it 20, 30 fold" — and didn't use it when the objection came. → **Better language:** *"Before I move on price — what does a funded $200K deal earn Armada, and how many reps are prospecting today? You get one deal a year out of this and the math isn't close. Let's establish that number first, then talk about the fee."* → **Why it matters:** every discount conceded before a value number exists becomes the new ceiling, and Armada is the first prospect where a real number was reachable.

- **Deflected the one objection that decides whether the product fits their book.** Bill's "truck driver with a fleet of two" is a direct challenge to signal coverage at the SME floor — where a $200K-average book actually lives — and Alek redirected it into a bankruptcy question and never came back. It's also the third independent surfacing of the same low-end gap (Michael/Providence, Stauss, now Bill). → **Better language:** *"That's the real limitation and I'm not going to talk around it. Public signal thins out at the very small end. Before our next call I'll run your buy box and tell you exactly how many of the companies that surface sit under $10M revenue — if that number's bad, you should know before you pay us."* → **Why it matters:** naming a limit and returning with data is the fastest credibility purchase available, and it converts demo #2 from a repeat demo into a delivered commitment.

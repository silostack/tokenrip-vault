## Strategy in one line

Ask one diagnostic question, let him propose, anchor once with a reason, trade instead of conceding, stop.

## Agenda (15–20 min)

**1. Open (2 min).** Thanks for the straight answer. "Heard the guys are on all three lists now, how's it landing?" Get the batch read: Trojan Battery, anything from weeks 2–3, verdicts coming?

**2. Diagnose (1 question).** "Was it the half point specifically, or anything tied to funded deals?" Then stop. This picks the branch.

**3. He proposes.** "So what does it look like on your end? You've got three weeks in the reps' hands, you've got a read." Silence. Let him name the number.
- **$1,500 or more:** yes. Stop talking.
- **Drifts to the old $3k:** yes. Stop talking.
- **$1,000 flat:** one counter, by branch below.

**4. Anchor once (by branch).**
- *"Just the percentage":* "Then keep something on funded, flat, paid by you, not the customer. Five grand when a name you marked working funds. Your idea from a couple weeks ago." 12-month clock stays.
- *"Nothing tied to deals":* "The thousand was priced with the upside behind it. We were taking the bet with you. If we're not in on the deals, it's a straight service, and the number for that is fifteen hundred." Then silence. One reason only.
  - Backup if he pushes (pick one): "Forty names a month, sourced, checked against your box, evidence attached. Under forty bucks a name." Or: "You're paying more than that for ZoomInfo Intent and you told me it's too late to the party."
  - Don't say you need to get paid or mention your costs. He's questioning the number, not whether you deserve money. Give him a reason the number changed.

**5. Trade, don't concede.** If he holds at $1,000, ask for one thing back. First choice: "Fine at a thousand if Oct through Dec is committed up front." If he balks: "Fine at a thousand. Two things back. You've said this is better than ZoomInfo Intent, let me use that line with your name on it. And once something funds, Wingspire's a customer I can name." He already said the quote on 09-02; you're asking him to sign it, not write it. Never give ground for free. Two asks reads as a trade, three reads as a shakedown.

**6. Mechanics (3 min).** Card: yes. Oct 1 start, September free. Month to month. Quarterly review stays (it's how we tune the list). Keep marking working / existing / pass.

**7. Close (2 min).** Restate the terms in one sentence. "I'll put that in an email tonight, reply 'works' and we're done." Get the card on the call or in the reply.

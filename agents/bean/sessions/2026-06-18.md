# Session — 2026-06-18

**Ideas touched**: workspace-brain, semantic-shared-memory-collaboration, mounted-agent-model, agentic-artifact-coordination, coordination-vs-collaboration

## What Was Explored

The concrete enablement of semantic search on workspaces ("anything put in gets embeddings; searches against the workspace are semantic"). What this unlocks for non-coder knowledge workers whose daily surface is Claude Cowork (or equivalent), inside one firm and across firms. The "git for everything non-coding" framing against the a16z spine. First-principles look at agentic collaboration: single-player AI is the current reality; what does genuine multi-player look like, and how is coordination (solved) different from collaboration (not solved) between sovereign parties on ambiguous joint work.

## What Emerged

- Semantic turns the 5k-file problem from "needs heroic human indexing or perfect names" into "the meaning is the address." Even perfect git sync across Cowork local FS would leave an agent helpless in a raw directory; semantic retrieval + agent-as-librarian removes the human from the retrieval loop.

- For two lawyers in the same firm on Cowork: each agent's work is informed by the shared matter brain without explicit handoffs. One drops or generates material; the other's agent surfaces the relevant slice on its own task because the query matches meaning, not path. "Fine tuning" becomes scoped semantic queries against zones or filters (Doctrine vs Signals, recency, provenance). The unit of collaboration becomes the corpus, not the document.

- Cross-company: each side keeps private brain + contributes to / retrieves from a scoped shared workspace. Agents choose what crosses the boundary. Neutrality is structural — neither firm will put the joint record inside the counterparty's model vendor's tenancy. The hard engineering surface is the private/shared boundary (scoped recall that doesn't leak via embeddings).

- The mounted agent separation is the enabler: memory lives on Tokenrip (imprint + workspace brain), harness is the user's chosen tool (Cowork, Codex, future). A Claude user and an OpenAI user literally share the same brain surface without common tooling.

- "Git for non-coding" works because code had an explicit graph (imports, compiler, line history). Knowledge work's graph is latent in the text. Embeddings recover candidate edges; actual retrieval + provenance recording makes them real. The non-coder doesn't maintain the taxonomy; the agent does the meaning-addressing and the consolidate loop promotes learning.

- Coordination vs collaboration sharpened as the filter for the category. Coordination = one principal's intent, decomposed and executed across agents (exists today in multi-agent frameworks + shared task state). Collaboration = multiple principals, divergent private context, implicit distributed knowledge too large to pass, joint artifact whose relevant slice must be retrieved on demand, with the retrieval itself recorded. The substrate has to support retrieval across trust boundaries, not just within one control plane.

- Unique capabilities that follow from first principles in the daily-AI-tool era: (1) harness-independent memory (the brain is not inside any one app's session or tenant); (2) semantic addressability for prose domains; (3) neutral ground between vendors and between organizations; (4) read-side provenance graph that accrues automatically from use; (5) stigmergy at scale — agents evolve shared artifacts as their primary coordination medium; (6) the accumulation happens as byproduct of people already using their preferred single-player surfaces.

- The a16z spine ("coding agents work because code has a memory; that record was both substrate and corpus") is completed by the recall layer. Without semantic retrieval inside the tools people actually use, the recorded why remains an archive. With it, and with the separation that lets any harness reach the same memory, operational domains get the missing piece.

## Named traps + countermeasures

- **Infrastructure-language trap** (8+ documented instances, both founders): buyers ask "what is it?" and get architecture. Fix: rehearsed 15-second plain-English answers + a demo to point at — a tooling fix, not a discipline fix (4/30–5/21)
- **Warmth-softening**: when a call goes *well*, that's peak leverage — Simon reads it as a moment to soften price/scope. Hold. (5/26, both traps pre-named in writing and still hit)
- **Answer-at-altitude**: substantively right, delivered as a four-item menu; lead with the one-sentence answer, depth on demand ("sandbox," 6/1)
- **Explain vs. extract**: on discovery/intel calls run 30% explain / 70% extract; before any call, write the 3 things to LEARN, not say (5/19)
- **Dopamine-order narration**: what Simon *leads with* in a weekly download reveals where attention sits; compare against value-order (5/31)
- **Affirming hands over the pen**: enthusiastic insiders author wedge/pricing/scope toward their own economics while founders receive-and-ratify; take domain input as gold, own the synthesis (5/29, 6/1)

## Diagnostics that work on Simon

- **Fact or inference — label it**; confirm the load-bearing operational fact before prescribing (6/8)
- **The wedge question** (Simon's own proven move): "what's the smallest commercial unit that requires the architecture to deliver?" (5/1)
- **Item vs. stream**: a one-off gray ask can be a gift; a recurring one is a contract (7/15)

- **Pain AND architecture**: a motion needs painkiller pain *and* architectural requirement — either alone fails (5/8)
- **Oscillation vs. verdict**: thesis change mid-test = destructive oscillation; channel reallocation off a clean null = discovery working (5/8, 5/18)
- **Pre-registered checkpoints beat vibes**: tests get a date + thresholds at adoption (e.g. the sprint's Aug-25 pain-voice count); hold the checkpoint even when energy has moved on (7/15)
- **"Getting ghosted" is victim framing** — reframe to "I didn't create urgency"; shifts locus of control back to the work (3/03)

Quintel — screen wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes of the v1 screens from the UX framework (quintel-ux-framework-2026-07-01.md). Grayscale and schematic on purpose — the point is layout, flow, and the “why-everywhere” signature, not visual polish. Data is synthetic but internally consistent across screens.

01 · Home

Prospects

The ranked worklist (table). Stat block, BLIND↔QUINTEL before/after, woven-in “what changed” digest, net-new fits, and the down-ranked Skip with its reason.
02 · The wow

Item Detail

The comprehension surface. “Scored against your 220-deal book,” Strengths + Checks tied to their own deals, resembles-your-deals, market activity with the reach-out-not-prediction disclaimer.
03 · The mirror

Profile

Quantified self-knowledge + the tunable fit model. Book at a glance, the weights the ranker uses, and a tuning prompt (question, not verdict).
04 · Track

Pipeline

Working leads by status (manual in v1). Won/Lost captures a reason — the deepest personalization signal.
Tiers: Call first Work Skip  — action instructions, not close-probability. Fit score (0–100) drives the sort; every row carries its “why.”
Demo arc: Mirror (Profile) → Apply (Prospects + Item Detail, lead with the before/after) → Fresh (what-changed) → live-tune. Pre-frame on screen: “a smarter cold-call list, not confirmed live deals.”

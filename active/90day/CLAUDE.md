## The three commitments (2026-07-15)

1. **Volume:** 20 dials + 25 LinkedIn connects per working day during the front-load. Calls before noon. Non-negotiable block.
2. **Kill list (dead while the sprint runs):** blog engine deployment · Tokenrip infra for the Quintel blog · HubSpot CRM integration for the demo.
3. **Iron rule:** the next step after every call costs the prospect something (money, a signed pilot, their data). Never free Simon build-hours.

## Definitions (use these exactly — they keep the scoreboard honest)

- **DM conversation:** a live conversation with someone who can sign or is one intro from the signer (owner / president / CEO / CCO / VP-or-Head of Originations).
- **Pain voice:** a decision-maker describing their own sourcing/deal-flow pain in their own words AND engaging on a path to dollars. (Enthusiasm ≠ pain. "Not flinching at the price" ≠ pain. Katharine 6/18 = the reference example.)
- **Funded:** money moved. Not verbal, not "loves it," not a signed-but-unfunded contract.

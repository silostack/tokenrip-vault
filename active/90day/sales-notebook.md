### Insight — founding-lender scarcity in outreach (2026-07-21, two-tier rule)

**Soft scarcity belongs in touch one; hard scarcity does not.**

- **Soft ("onboarding a few more lenders on founding terms") — YES in touch one.** Does three honest jobs at once: reason-why-now, signals favorable pricing without a number, pre-frames the "who else uses it?" question as intentional early access rather than a weakness. True (offer is capped at 5). This is the version in Track C v1.1.
- **Hard ("only a few spots left" / countdown) — NO in touch one.** Scarcity pressure only works on existing desire; a stranger has none, so it reads as the single most recognizable spam pattern and gets you deleted. Save it for touch 2+ once there's engagement, or a call where they're wobbling on timing — there the §7 line ("first-mover pricing ends at five") converts real desire into a date.
- **Honesty gate before using hard scarcity anywhere:** is the 5-seat cap real in Simon + Alek's heads? Will price/terms actually change at customer 6? If you'd quietly extend founding terms to customer 8, dial the language back now — a scarcity claim you don't enforce trains you to wobble on price (Simon's documented trap).

# AICAP Retainer — Options Tear Sheet (internal baseline for the Stephanie conversation)

*2026-07-15 · Ballparks to anchor against, not a proposal doc. Governing artifact: the signed [[product/aicap/aicap-validation-mvp-sow-2026-06-22|Validation MVP SOW]] — $12K fixed ($11K net of discovery), ~7 weeks, acceptance against de-identified samples. Now week 2; her scope realization is growing, hospital calls going well, "demo-ready" urgency arriving — and she floated the retainer unprompted. Maximum-leverage moment.*

## The principle that resolves everything

**She bought the deliverable, not the weeks.** The $12K bought the SOW's acceptance criteria; the 7 weeks was a delivery estimate and schedule risk on that scope is ours (that's what fixed-price means). Two rules, no exceptions:

1. **Never bill more for SOW scope** — even if it runs past week 7. No re-trading signed work.
2. **Never do beyond-SOW scope for free** — effective immediately, not at week 7. **The retainer trigger is scope, not calendar.**

| Lane | What | Money |
|---|---|---|
| **Lane 1 — SOW build** | The five surfaces to acceptance, weekly demos, current pace | Funded. Done deal. Zero new dollars. |
| **Lane 2 — the retainer** | Everything beyond the acceptance criteria: the Jul-9 brain-dump backlog, demo-readiness for her hospital calls (SOW explicitly fences this out — de-identified samples, one baseline config, no production polish), priority/speed beyond the SOW's promised cadence, async support, the running "one more thing" queue | **The retainer — starts the day she says yes; naturally continues past SOW acceptance** |

**Major new modules get quoted as separate fixed milestones even under the retainer.**

## Pre-call move (do this before the conversation)

Triage her brain-dump + demo-ready asks against the SOW acceptance criteria into three buckets:
- **Covered** — no charge; say so generously.
- **Beyond scope** — Lane 2; this list IS the retainer justification, in her own asks.
- **Ambiguous** — deliberately include one or two free and *say so out loud* ("borderline — I'm including it"). Goodwill made visible instead of leaked silently.

Walking in with the inventory converts a negotiation into a transparent review — the research differentiator applied to our own deal.

## Scope rubric — SOW-in vs. Lane 2 (run every item through four gates, in order)

1. **Surface test.** Does it live inside the five surfaces + admin view? A new surface, user type, or output → Lane 2. Deepens an existing surface → next gate.
2. **Baseline test.** Is it the *one baseline configuration locked at kickoff*, or a variant/expansion of it? Her compliance checklist arriving and getting calibrated **is the SOW working as designed** — it's Week 2's line item and listed under "What AICAP provides." So most of the brain-dump = **in scope, no charge, say so.** What's out: second configurations, per-hospital bars, state variants beyond the baseline, question banks growing well past the kickoff assumption. The boundary is *calibrating the baseline* (in) vs. *expanding beyond it* (Lane 2).
3. **Acceptance test.** Does the work serve passing the seven acceptance criteria on the agreed sample set — or does it serve her hospital demos? Acceptance → in. Demo polish, production hardening, real-data readiness, per-hospital config → Lane 2 (the SOW names all of these out of scope explicitly).
4. **Recurrence test — item vs. stream.** A one-off gray item can be a gift. A gray item that shows up weekly is a *stream*, and **streams are never gifts** — they're Lane 2 by definition, no matter how small each instance.

**Tie-break policy (the generosity you want, made safe):** ambiguous → **her favor, out loud, logged.** "This one's borderline — I'm including it" costs nothing and buys relationship capital; silent inclusion reads as scope and sets precedent. Keep a one-line goodwill ledger in the project tracker, capped at **~half a day a week**. When the cap hits: "I'm past my goodwill budget this week — adding this to the retainer queue." Friendly, structural, no negotiation. And never gift a *category* ("compliance tweaks are included") — only *items*.

What the cut *means*: every −20% tier lands at ~$86/hr — i.e., **the SOW's design-partner discount rolling forward silently**, which directly contradicts the "one-time price tied to the partnership agreement" story. At Standard, the gap is **$1,500/mo = $18K/yr.** If you want a planned concession in your pocket, fine — but a concession must *buy* something: $6,000 in exchange for a 3-month minimum, or a quarter prepaid, or the partnership agreement signed with reference + case-study rights. Never open at $6K, and never drift there because the conversation felt warm.

## Rate logic (every number in this is true — say it without flinching)

- SOW effective rate: $12K / ~7 weeks ≈ **$70–85/hr effective** — a one-time, explicitly-discounted design-partner price.
- Standard retainer tier (~2 d/wk, $7.5K/mo) ≈ **$108/hr — restoring the standard rate, not a raise.** Market for a senior product engineer shipping weekly: $150–200/hr.
- A retainer prices **priority and continuity**, not hours: she's selling to hospitals *now*; guaranteed access to the person who builds her product is what protects her demo windows.

## The un-papered partnership agreement (framing gift — bring it)

The SOW discount was given *against* design-partner consideration (reference/case-study rights, the "partnership agreement" she asked to table) — never drafted. Fold it in: *"The $12K was a design-partner price tied to the partnership agreement we still owe each other. Let's do both now — the retainer at standard rate, and I'll draft the partnership doc with the reference rights this week."* Closes both loose ends; retroactively justifies the discount instead of letting it silently become the price level.

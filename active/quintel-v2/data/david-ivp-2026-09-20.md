2. Majority sell **used** equipment: specialty trucks, vehicles, cranes.
3. Fewer than 5 locations.
4. Most locations in smaller cities and towns.
5. Over 60% list on Facebook Marketplace or an online marketplace: MachineryTrader, IronPlanet, Machineryline, EquipmentTrader, Equipt, SurplusRecord (he linked the packaging-equipment category).
